## Google ML Kit and Google Translate

Glance depends on Google's ML Kit text recognition and on-device translation libraries, distributed through Google's Maven repository. The application packages the Latin, Chinese, Devanagari, Japanese, and Korean recognizers. Translation models are downloaded by the SDK when a language is first used.

- [ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms)
- [On-device translation usage guidelines](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/language/translation/translation-terms)
- [Google Translate attribution requirements](https://cloud.google.com/translate/attribution)
- [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
